      How to play Mini

      Fill the grid so every across and down answer matches its clue.

      • Tap a square to select it. Tap it again to switch between across and down.
      • Type to fill letters. The arrows next to the clue move between clues.
      • Check marks wrong letters in red. Reveal word fills in the current answer.

      The clock starts when you start. A new Mini comes out every day at midnight.
