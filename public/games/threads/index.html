How to play Threads

Find the theme words hidden in the grid. Today's theme is shown at the top.

  • Drag across letters, or tap them one by one, to spell a word. Then tap the last letter again (or lift your finger) to submit.
  • Letters must touch, including diagonally. Words can bend in any direction.
  • Theme words fill the whole board, and no letter is used twice.
  • Find 3 other real words (4+ letters) to earn a hint, which outlines the letters of one theme word.

A new board comes out every day at midnight.
