How to play Quads

Find groups of four words that share something in common.

  • Tap four words, then Submit to check your guess.
  • Every puzzle has exactly one answer. Watch out for words that seem to fit more than one group!
  • You can make 4 mistakes before the game ends.

Groups get trickier from yellow to green to blue to purple. Example: BAT, RACKET, CLUB, PADDLE are things you hit with.
