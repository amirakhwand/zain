How to play Domino

Place every domino on the board so that each colored region follows its rule.

  • Tap a domino below the board, then tap a square to place it. Tap a placed domino to rotate it.
  • A number means the pips in that region add up to it. < and > mean less or more than it.
  • = means every square in the region shows the same number. ≠ means they're all different.
  • Squares with no badge can hold anything.

Each puzzle has exactly one answer. New easy, medium and hard boards every day.
