How to play Hunch

Guess the hidden word in 6 tries. Each guess must be a real 5-letter word.

After each guess the tiles change color:

ZANYS

Green: right letter, right spot.

BRAIN

Gold: in the word, but in a different spot.

CLOUD

Grey: not in the word at all.

A new word comes out every day at midnight.
