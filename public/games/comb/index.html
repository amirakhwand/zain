    How to play Comb

    Make words using the 7 letters in the comb.

    • Words must be at least 4 letters long.
    • Every word must include the center letter.
    • Letters can be used more than once.
    • No proper nouns or hyphenated words.

    Scoring

    • 4-letter words are worth 1 point.
    • Longer words earn 1 point per letter.
    • Every puzzle has at least one word that uses all 7 letters. It's worth 7 bonus points.

    Tap a letter or type on your keyboard. A new comb comes out every day at midnight.

    Rankings

    Ranks are based on a share of today's total possible points.
